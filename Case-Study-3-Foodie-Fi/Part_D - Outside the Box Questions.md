# Question 1

## Question
How would you calculate the rate of growth for Foodie-Fi?

## Answer

The growth rate of Foodie-Fi can be measured by tracking the number of active customers on a monthly basis and comparing it with the previous month's active customer count.

A month-over-month growth rate can then be calculated using the formula:

**Growth Rate (%) = ((Current Month Active Customers − Previous Month Active Customers) / Previous Month Active Customers) × 100**

This provides a clear view of customer acquisition trends and whether the platform is growing, stagnating, or declining over time.

# Question 2

## Question
What key metrics would you recommend Foodie-Fi management to track over time to assess performance of their overall business?

## Answer

Foodie-Fi management should track the following key metrics over time:

- **Customer growth rate:** Measures the month-over-month or year-over-year change in the number of active customers.
- **Churn rate:** Tracks the percentage of customers who cancel their subscriptions during a given period.
- **Trial conversion rate:** Measures the percentage of trial customers who move to a paid subscription plan.
- **Plan upgrade and downgrade rates:** Shows how frequently customers move between Basic Monthly, Pro Monthly and Pro Annual plans.
- **Average time to upgrade:** Measures how long customers typically take to move from a lower-priced plan to a higher-priced plan.
- **Monthly recurring revenue:** Tracks the recurring revenue generated from active monthly subscriptions.
- **Average revenue per customer:** Measures the average subscription revenue generated by each active customer.
- **Customer retention rate:** Tracks the percentage of customers who remain subscribed over a specific period.

Together, these metrics would help management evaluate customer acquisition, conversion, retention, subscription behaviour and revenue performance.

# Question 3

## Question
What are some key customer journeys or experiences that you would analyse further to improve customer retention?

## Answer
I would analyse the customer journey from free trial to paid subscription to identify conversion barriers, study upgrade paths from Basic Monthly to Pro Monthly and Pro Annual to understand what drives higher-value subscriptions, examine churned customers to identify common cancellation patterns, and compare the behaviour of long-term retained customers to determine which experiences contribute to higher customer retention.

# Question 4

## Question
If the Foodie-Fi team were to create an exit survey shown to customers who wish to cancel their subscription, what questions would you include in the survey?

## Answer
The following questions could be included in an exit survey to better understand why customers are cancelling their subscription:

1. What is the primary reason for cancelling your subscription?
2. How satisfied were you with your overall experience with Foodie-Fi?
3. Did you face any issues with the content, pricing, features, or user experience?
4. Is there any feature or improvement that would have encouraged you to continue your subscription?
5. Would you consider subscribing to Foodie-Fi again in the future? If yes, under what circumstances?
6. Please share any additional feedback or suggestions that could help us improve our service

# Question 5

## Question
What business levers could the Foodie-Fi team use to reduce the customer churn rate? How would you validate the effectiveness of your ideas?

## Answer

Foodie-Fi could reduce customer churn by implementing several business initiatives, including:

- Introducing additional subscription plans (such as 3-month or 6-month plans) for customers who find annual subscriptions expensive but prefer longer commitments than monthly billing.
- Enabling AutoPay to provide a seamless renewal experience and reduce missed renewals.
- Offering personalized discounts or retention offers to customers who are likely to cancel.
- Extending trial periods or providing limited-time promotional offers to encourage customers to experience more value before making a purchase decision.
- Improving content recommendations based on customer preferences to increase engagement.

The effectiveness of these initiatives can be validated by monitoring key metrics such as churn rate, customer retention rate, trial-to-paid conversion rate, subscription upgrade rate, and average customer lifetime. A/B testing can also be used to compare customer behaviour before and after implementing each initiative.
